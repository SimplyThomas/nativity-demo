Parish bookstore

Books, icons and things for the home

The bookstore keeps a small, carefully chosen stock: Scripture and prayer books, icons for the home, incense, candles and prayer ropes. Order here and collect after Divine Liturgy on Sunday — nothing is posted, and nothing is paid for until you have it in your hands.

How collection works
1. Reserve what you would like below.
2. We set it aside and email you a confirmation.
3. Collect and pay at the bookstore table after Liturgy, in the narthex.

When the table is open

Open every Sunday after the Divine Liturgy. Parishioners and visitors are both welcome to browse.

Looking for something else

Most Orthodox titles can be ordered in within a week or two. Ask at the table, or write to the parish office.

New to Orthodoxy

If you are not sure where to begin, say so. There are two or three books we usually put in the hands of someone visiting for the first time, and there is no obligation to buy any of them.
