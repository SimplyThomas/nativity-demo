Nativity of the Theotokos Greek Orthodox Church
Adult Baptism & Chrismation checklist
Last updated: Not yet issued

For an adult being received into the Orthodox Church, and for their godparent.

Everything an adult needs to see to before being received into the Church, in the order it comes up. Work through it with your godparent, and bring anything you are unsure about to Fr. John.

Structure ready — requirements awaiting Fr. JohnThe headings are here so you can see what the document will cover. The requirements under them are Fr. John's to set, and are not on the page until he has set them.

1Before the service

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What has to be arranged, agreed or completed beforehand, and how far ahead.

2Your godparent or sponsor

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Who may serve as your sponsor, what the parish asks of them, and whether Fr. John approves the choice.

3What you need to bring

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: The full list, so that nothing is discovered on the morning.

4Clothing

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What to wear, what changes of clothing to bring, and what the parish provides.

5Cross

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Whether a baptismal cross is needed, who provides it, and whether it is blessed beforehand.

6Candles

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: How many, what kind, and who brings them.

7Towels and linens

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What is needed, who provides it, and what happens to it afterwards.

8Paperwork the parish needs

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Forms, records and dates the office needs, and by when. Nothing is named here until the office confirms it.

9Confession and spiritual preparation

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Fr. John's guidance. Whether a first Confession comes before reception, and how to prepare for it.

10Fasting and preparing for Communion

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Fr. John's guidance, including how it is adjusted for health, pregnancy, age or medication.

11The day of the service

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: When to arrive, where to go, who will be there, and how long it lasts.

12After your reception

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What comes next — Communion, Confession, your name day, and anything the parish asks of the newly received.

Questions? The parish office — (540) 548-2665, office@ntgoc.org, Tuesday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
