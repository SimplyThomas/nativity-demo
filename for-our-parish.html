A hand to it

Serve

Almost everything that happens at Nativity happens because somebody volunteered to do it. The ministries page lists what the parish actually runs; it is the place to start, and it is kept in one place so it cannot fall out of step with a second list here.

Nothing has been added to that list to make it look longer. If a ministry you expect is missing, it is missing because nobody has confirmed it exists.
