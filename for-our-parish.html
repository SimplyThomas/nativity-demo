Stay connected

Parish life carries on through the week — a service on a weekday evening, a Philoptochos meeting, a work day at the church, a family in need of help. Most of it is passed on by word of mouth on a Sunday, which works well if you were there and not at all if you weren't.

Two things would close that gap. Neither exists today; both are here for Fr. John and the Council to consider.

In the meantime, The Light is published monthly, and the parish posts to Facebook and YouTube.

Serve & get involved

Every ministry of this parish is run by parishioners, and every one of them welcomes newcomers. You do not need to have been here long, and you do not need to be asked twice. Speak to a ministry at coffee hour, or write to the parish office and we will put you in touch.

The ministries page is the full list — Sunday School, GOYA and JOY, the Ladies' Philoptochos Society, the Dance Troupe, AHEPA, the Parish Council, and those who serve in the divine services.

Stewardship & giving

Stewardship is one of the ways we offer back to God from the gifts He has entrusted to us, and take part in the life and work of our parish. It is not dues: it is the offering by which the parish exists — the divine services, the building, the Sunday School, and the charity given away in the parish's name.

Registering your family is the first step to being counted as a steward, and to joining any ministry.

Ways to give

Online giving and memorial donations go through the parish's existing Square pages. Cheques may be posted to the parish office.

Children & families

During the school year, children attending Sunday School leave for class after Holy Communion has been served and stay in class until coffee hour, when they rejoin their families in the hall.

GOYA and JOY are the parish's youth ministries. Details of what each is doing this term come from the ministries themselves; ask at coffee hour or write to the parish office.
