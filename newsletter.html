Contributing to The Light

Parish announcements, photographs and articles can be sent to newsletter@ntgoc.org. Please ask the people in a photograph before sending it, and ask a parent separately for any photograph that prominently features a child — the same rule the parish applies to photographs on the website.

The deadline, the publication schedule and who edits The Light are not recorded anywhere we could source. They belong on this page once someone confirms them.
