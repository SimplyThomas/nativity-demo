Parish ministries

The parish is what its people do together

Every ministry below welcomes newcomers, and none of them require you to have been here long. Ask at coffee hour, or write to the parish office. Descriptions are kept short on purpose — what each ministry is doing this term comes from the ministry itself. Where a name is given, it is the person the parish lists for that ministry.

Youth

Sunday School

Classes for the children of the parish during the school year. Children leave for class after Holy Communion has been served and stay until coffee hour.

Michael Euripides
