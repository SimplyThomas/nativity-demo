Parish ministries

The parish is what its people do together

Every ministry below welcomes newcomers, and none of them require you to have been here long. Ask at coffee hour, or write to the parish office.

Youth

Sunday School

Classes for ages 4 through high school, meeting after Holy Communion from September through May. New students may join any week.

Youth

GOYA

Greek Orthodox Youth of America, grades 7–12. Monthly meetings, service projects, basketball, and the Metropolis summer camp.

Youth

JOY

Junior Orthodox Youth, grades 3–6. Crafts, outings and service, with parents very much included.

Service

Ladies' Philoptochos Society

“Friends of the poor.” The parish's charitable arm — local food assistance, hospital visits, and national Archdiocese relief work.

Culture

Dance Troupe

Traditional dance from across Greece, rehearsing through the year and performing at the festival. All ages, no experience needed.

Fellowship

AHEPA

The American Hellenic Educational Progressive Association — scholarships, veterans' work and community service.

Governance

Parish Council

Elected stewards responsible for the temporal affairs of the parish, serving alongside the presiding priest.

Worship

Choir & Chanters

Byzantine chant and choral singing at every Liturgy. Readers and altar servers are also always needed.

Register your family

The first step to joining any ministry — and to being counted as a steward of the parish.
