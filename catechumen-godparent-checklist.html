Nativity of the Theotokos Greek Orthodox Church
Godparent checklist for Baptism & Chrismation
Last updated: Not yet issued

For the godparent or sponsor, at an adult reception or a child's baptism.

What is being asked of you, in the order it comes up. If any of it is difficult — including the cost of what a godparent traditionally provides — say so to Fr. John rather than to the family. It is an ordinary conversation and he would far rather have it.

Structure ready — requirements awaiting Fr. JohnThe headings are here so you can see what the document will cover. The requirements under them are Fr. John's to set, and are not on the page until he has set them.

1Before you say yes

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Who may serve as a sponsor at this parish, what standing is asked, and whether Fr. John approves the choice. This is the section a person needs before they answer, not after.

2Before the service

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What to arrange, what to speak to Fr. John about, and how far ahead.

3What you provide

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Exactly what the godparent brings or pays for, and what the parish or the family provides instead.

4What you do during the service

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Where you stand, what you hold, what you say, and what you are handed.

5After the Baptism or Chrismation

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What happens immediately afterwards, and anything the parish asks of the godparent in the following weeks.

6The ongoing relationship

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What the parish hopes for from a godparent in the years afterwards, put plainly enough to be useful.

Questions? The parish office — (540) 548-2665, office@ntgoc.org, Tuesday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
