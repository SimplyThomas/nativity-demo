Hall rental

The parish hall is available to the community

Our hall seats roughly 200 and is used for baptisms, memorials, receptions, meetings and community gatherings. It has a full commercial kitchen, tables and chairs, a sound system, and parking on site.

Rental is open to parishioners and to the wider Fredericksburg community. Rates differ for the two, and a refundable deposit is required.

Capacity≈ 200 seated
KitchenFull commercial
ParkingOn-site, free
BookingThrough the parish office
