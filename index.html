Welcome
“Welcome one another, therefore, just as Christ has welcomed you, for the glory of God.”
Romans 15:7

For nearly two thousand years, the Orthodox Church has proclaimed the apostolic faith and gathered around the Eucharist in worship. That same life of prayer continues here in Fredericksburg.

If you are encountering Orthodoxy for the first time, you are welcome to visit. You do not need to know the prayers, understand when to stand, or know what to do beforehand. Simply come. We would be glad to have you with us.

The Fredericksburg Greek Festival

Three days of food, music and dancing on the church grounds, run by the parish and open to the whole city. For many of our neighbours it is the first time they walk through our doors — and the church is open for tours all weekend.
