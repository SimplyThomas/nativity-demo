Come and see.

Nativity of the Theotokos is a Greek Orthodox Christian parish in Fredericksburg, Virginia. Whether you are Orthodox, exploring the ancient Christian faith, or simply visiting, we welcome you to join us in prayer and worship.

Welcome
“Welcome one another, therefore, just as Christ has welcomed you, for the glory of God.”
Romans 15:7

For nearly two thousand years, the Orthodox Church has proclaimed the apostolic faith and gathered around the Eucharist in worship. That same life of prayer continues here in Fredericksburg.

If you are encountering Orthodoxy for the first time, you are welcome to visit. You do not need to know the prayers, understand when to stand, or know what to do beforehand. Simply come. We would be glad to have you with us.

Find your place at Nativity

The life of the parish is something you take part in rather than read about. Any one of these is a place to begin.

Worship The services through the week, the feast days, and the sacramental life of the Church. Learn What the Orthodox Church believes, how the faith is taught, and what becoming Orthodox involves. Serve Practical ways to help, however much time you have to give. Connect Coffee hour in the parish hall, and the ordinary company of one another. Care Offer practical help to a household that needs it — or ask for it yourself. Traditions Learn a tradition, teach one, prepare for a feast, and take your part in it.

Sample — this is how an urgent notice appears

An urgent announcement sorts to the front of this grid and takes the red rule, so it is not read as ordinary parish business. It is meant for a weather closure, a service moved at short notice, or a death in the parish family. Set "urgent": true on an entry in data/parish-announcements.json.

Announcements run newest first, each under a label saying what kind of notice it is, and each one can carry a date after which it comes down by itself rather than piling up. Proposed — what this space is for

This space is built to carry the week's parish announcements and practical needs, as the parish begins publishing them: a service time changed at short notice, a volunteer needed, flowers for a feast, a meal for a household, a class with places left. Whatever is posted appears here and on For our parish, from one file, so the two pages cannot drift apart. The standing ways to help are gathered on Get involved.

No real parish announcement has been written yet. What appears above is rendered from the announcements file as it stands, and nothing has been invented to fill the space.

The Fredericksburg Greek Festival

The parish runs the Fredericksburg Greek Festival, and it is open to the whole city. For many of our neighbours it is the first time they walk through our doors. The dates and the details are kept at fredgreek.org.
