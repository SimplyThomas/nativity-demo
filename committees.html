Parish Council

The committees that do the work

The Parish Council works through six standing committees. Each one is open to any parishioner willing to help. If one of them interests you, write to its lead or speak with them after Divine Liturgy.

Building and Grounds / Facilities

Parish Council Leads: Council Member and Council Member

Oversees the care of our church buildings, grounds, and parish property, including maintenance, repairs, safety, security, and long-term facility planning.

Worship / Liturgical

Leads: Fr. John and Council Member

Supports the reverent celebration of divine services by helping coordinate liturgical volunteers, supplies, and other worship needs under the guidance of our priest.

Fellowship / Hospitality

Lead: Council Member

Strengthens Christian fellowship by coordinating coffee hours, parish meals, visitor hospitality, and social gatherings.

Education / Catechesis

Leads: Council Member and Council Member

Supports Sunday School, Bible studies, catechumen instruction, educational resources, and other opportunities for Orthodox Christian formation.

Youth and Family

Leads: Council Member and Council Member

Develops activities, programs, and service opportunities that support the spiritual growth of children, teenagers, young adults, and families.

Fundraising and Events

Lead: Council Member

Coordinates fundraising initiatives and major parish events that support the mission and ministries of our church.

Who serves on them

Each committee is led by members of the Parish Council, who are elected by the stewards of the parish and serve alongside Fr. John. The Council roster is on the About page. You do not have to be on the Council to join a committee — most of the people doing this work are not.

Every lead above reads “Council Member” because the current roster has not been supplied. Please send it and the names will be set here and on the About page together.
