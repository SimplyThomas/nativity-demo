Parish life

Care & Support

A parish looks after its own. Two things happen on this page and they are of equal weight: offering help to a household that needs it, and asking for help when your own household does. Neither is the lesser way to be here.

Offer help Ask for help
When someone needs a hand

Care for one another

There are seasons when a home-cooked meal means more than we realise.

When someone in our parish welcomes a new baby, goes in for surgery, is unwell or recovering, has lost someone they love, or simply needs a little extra support for a while, the parish family can help carry it. Offering to cook is one way. Letting someone else cook for you is another, and it is just as much a part of this.

Provide a meal

See whether a parish family needs meals just now, and choose a day when you are able to cook. One dinner, on one evening, is a real help — you are not signing up for a fortnight.

Proposed — no form handler yet

Request meal support

Could your household use some help with meals right now? Please tell us. Asking for help is welcome here — it is what a parish family is for, and most of us are on both sides of it sooner or later.

A request sent from here would go privately to the parish ministry coordinator. Nothing would be posted publicly, and nobody else in the parish would be told before somebody had spoken with you.

Request support
What kind of support would be helpful?

However much or little you would like to say. Allergies, how many people, which days, or nothing at all.

Preferred way to contact you

Thank you for asking. Someone from the parish will be in touch with you privately.

Nothing is transmitted. There is no form handler behind this page, so pressing the button shows you the reply you would receive and stops there. Today, the route that works is the parish office.

How can our parish family help?

Meals are only one of the ways we look after one another. If you are facing something and are not sure whom to ask, please reach out anyway.

You do not need to know which ministry handles it, or which person. Tell the office what is going on and they will find the right next step. If what you need is a conversation with Fr. John, the office will arrange it.
