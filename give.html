Stewardship

Giving, and what it pays for

Stewardship is not dues. It is the offering by which the parish exists: the priest's salary, the utilities, the Sunday School materials, the charity the Philoptochos gives away, and the building itself.

Visitors are never expected to give. If you are new, put nothing in the tray and enjoy the coffee.

Ways to give

Give online Memorial donation 2026 stewardship pledge Family registration form

Cheques may be mailed to the parish office at 12326 Spotswood Furnace Rd, Fredericksburg, VA 22407.

Building projects

The parish maintains an ongoing programme of capital work on the church and grounds. Plans, budgets and progress reports are published so stewards can see where their offerings go.
