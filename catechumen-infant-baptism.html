Nativity of the Theotokos Greek Orthodox Church
Infant & child Baptism checklist
Last updated: Not yet issued

For parents arranging the Baptism of a child, and for the godparent.

What the parish asks of parents and godparents preparing for the Baptism of a child. Anything you are unsure about, ask the parish office — it is a better use of a telephone call than a guess.

Structure ready — requirements awaiting Fr. JohnThe headings are here so you can see what the document will cover. The requirements under them are Fr. John's to set, and are not on the page until he has set them.

The parish's existing baptism checklist is out of date. It has NOT been copied here, and nothing on that page was taken from it. It needs Fr. John's review before anything is published.

1Before the baptism

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What to arrange with the parish and the office, and how far ahead a date is set.

2The godparent

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Who may serve as godparent for a child, what the parish asks of them, and whether Fr. John approves the choice.

3What to bring

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: The full list for the family and for the godparent.

4Baptismal garments and clothing

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What the child wears, what to bring for afterwards, and who provides it.

5Cross

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Whether a cross is needed, who provides it, and whether it is blessed beforehand.

6Candles

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: How many, what kind, and who brings them.

7Towels, linens and oil

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: What is needed, who provides it, and what happens to it afterwards.

8Paperwork the parish needs

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Forms, records and names the office needs, and by when.

9The day of the service

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: When to arrive, where to go, and how long it lasts. Photography, if the parish has a practice about it.

10After the baptism

Content needed from Fr. JohnThis section will say: Communion for the newly baptised, the baptismal certificate, and anything else the parish asks of the family.

Questions? The parish office — (540) 548-2665, office@ntgoc.org, Tuesday to Friday 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.
