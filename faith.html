Our Faith

The Church of the Apostles, still here

Orthodox Christianity is not a denomination that broke away from something. It is the continuous life of the Church founded at Pentecost, holding the same faith, the same sacraments and the same worship across twenty centuries.

That continuity is what a visitor feels first: the hymns, the icons, the fasting seasons, the shape of the year. None of it is nostalgia. It is the ordinary practice of a Church that never stopped.

The pages below are the parish's introduction to that life. If you would rather ask a person than read, that is always the better option — Fr. John is glad to talk.
